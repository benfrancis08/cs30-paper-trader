# Beta Test
## Experienced:
### What they liked:
- Easy to understand and very user-friendly
- Ability to see the live price of all stocks and their graph
- Having their own server at home that runs code
- Able to see transaction history/owned shares
### What to improve:
- Line that shows where you bought and where you sold stock
- Slight improvement to ui like colours and logos for the types of stock to make it more aesthetically pleasing
- Make it easier to sell by automatically fetching bought shares to sell what you have, without having to type in the exact price/share
- Add more stocks to invest in to fill up the left side of the page

## Non-Experienced:
### What they liked:
- easy to use, like the graphs
### What to improve:
- once click enter to buy or sell shares the pop up window does not go away until you hit the buy or sell buttons
- it would be beneficial to show what your purchase price is compared to current value in the summary of transactions and share amount table
